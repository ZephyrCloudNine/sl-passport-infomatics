🇱🇰 Sri Lanka E-Passport

The Renewed Drive: Sri Lanka has launched an accelerated drive to deploy international-standard biometric e-passports, moving away from legacy paper documents.

Why It's Critical: Securing these upgrades is vital to comply with global ICAO security frameworks, combat document forgery, and modernise border controls. Crucially, it upgrades the power of the Sri Lankan passport—enabling seamless airport clearance, unlocking automated e-gates worldwide, and easing travel for the diaspora and global workforce.
